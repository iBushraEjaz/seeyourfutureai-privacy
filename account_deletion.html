Account Deletion Request

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data in See Your Future AI.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly within the app by following these steps:

1 Open the See Your Future AI app on your device.
2 Sign in to your account using your email and password or Google account.
3 Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
4 Scroll down to the "Delete Account" option.
5 Tap "Delete My Account".
6 Confirm by tapping "Delete" in the confirmation dialog.

What Data is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Account Information: Your email address, name, and authentication credentials.
  • Profile Data: Your name and Firestore profile settings.

Deletion Timeline

Your account will be disabled immediately upon deletion. All associated data will be permanently removed within 72 hours. During this period, your account cannot be accessed.

Important Notes

  • If you delete your account, you must wait 42 hours before you can create a new account with the same email address.
  • If you signed in with Google, your Google account will not be deleted — only the See Your Future AI account mapping is removed.

Alternative: Request via Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us. Please include:

  • Your registered email address
  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"

Email: seeyourfutureai.app@gmail.com

App: See Your Future AI

Expected Response Time: Within 48 hours
